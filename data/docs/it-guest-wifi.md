# Office Wi-Fi Networks

Owner: IT Department · Applies to: all Corvane Labs office locations

## Employee Network

The Corvane-Secure network is for company-managed laptops only. Devices connect automatically using a certificate installed by the device management system, so no password is needed. If your laptop does not join Corvane-Secure, restart it while connected to the office network by cable so the certificate can renew.

## Guest Network

Visitors and personal devices use the Corvane-Guest network. The guest password rotates every Monday at 6:00 AM local time and is shown on the screens at reception. Guest access is limited to the internet; it cannot reach internal systems, printers, or the VPN. Guest sessions expire after 24 hours.

## Personal Devices

Employees may connect personal phones and tablets to Corvane-Guest only. Connecting personal devices to Corvane-Secure is not allowed, even if you know a way to do it.

## Reporting Problems

If the Wi-Fi is down for more than a few people in an office, post in the #it-status Slack channel. For problems affecting only your device, open an IT service desk ticket and include the office location and floor.
