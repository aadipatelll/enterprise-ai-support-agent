# Performance Reviews

Owner: People Team · Applies to: all employees who have completed 90 days of employment

## Review Cycle

Corvane Labs runs performance reviews twice a year, in April and October. Each cycle includes a self-review, feedback from two to four peers, and a manager review. Employees hired less than 90 days before the cycle starts are skipped until the next cycle.

## Ratings

Managers assign one of four ratings: Exceeds Expectations, Meets Expectations, Partially Meets Expectations, or Does Not Meet Expectations. Ratings are calibrated across each department before they are shared with employees to keep them fair and consistent.

## Promotions and Compensation

Promotions are considered only during the October cycle. Merit salary increases take effect on January 1 and are based on the October rating. Managers submit promotion cases with written evidence against the next level's expectations in the career ladder.

## Disagreeing With a Review

If you disagree with your review, first discuss it with your manager. If it is not resolved, you may submit a written response in Workday within 14 days, which your skip-level manager and HR business partner will review.
