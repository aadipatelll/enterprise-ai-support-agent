# Device Returns and Lost Equipment

Owner: IT Department · Applies to: all employees with company-issued equipment

## Lost or Stolen Devices

Report a lost or stolen laptop or phone within 24 hours by calling the IT service desk at extension 4357 and posting in the #security-help Slack channel. IT will lock and remotely wipe the device. Theft must also be reported to local police, and the police report number added to the IT ticket.

## Returning Equipment When Leaving

Departing employees must return their laptop, charger, security key, and any monitors within 10 business days of their last day. Remote employees receive a prepaid shipping box from IT. Equipment that is not returned is deducted from the final paycheck where local law allows.

## Damaged Equipment

Accidental damage is repaired at no cost to the employee for the first incident in a 12-month period. A second incident in the same period requires manager approval before repair. Never attempt to repair a company device yourself or take it to a third-party repair shop.

## Data on Returned Devices

Back up any work files to Google Drive before returning a device. All returned devices are securely wiped and either redeployed or recycled through a certified partner.
