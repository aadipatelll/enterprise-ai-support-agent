# Acceptable Use Policy

Owner: Security Team · Applies to: all company devices, accounts, and networks

## Company Devices

Company laptops and phones are for work. Limited personal use, such as checking personal email or browsing news, is acceptable if it does not interfere with work or violate any policy. Do not let family members or friends use company devices. Do not disable the endpoint protection agent, firewall, or disk encryption.

## AI Tools

Employees may use the approved enterprise AI assistant for work tasks. Never paste Confidential or Restricted data, including source code and customer data, into consumer AI tools or unapproved websites. Report any accidental disclosure to the Security team immediately.

## Prohibited Activities

Prohibited activities include installing pirated software, mining cryptocurrency, accessing illegal content, running network scanners without Security approval, and using personal cloud storage such as Dropbox for company files.

## Monitoring

Company devices and accounts are monitored for security purposes, including endpoint activity and network traffic. Employees should have no expectation of privacy on company systems, except where local law requires otherwise. Violations may result in disciplinary action.
