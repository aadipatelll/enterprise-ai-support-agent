# Paid Time Off Policy

Owner: People Team · Applies to: full-time employees in the United States

## Accrual

Full-time employees accrue 20 days of paid time off (PTO) per year, earned at 1.67 days per month. After five years of service, accrual increases to 25 days per year. Part-time employees accrue PTO in proportion to their scheduled hours.

## Requesting Time Off

Request PTO in Workday at least two weeks in advance for absences longer than three days. Shorter absences need at least two business days of notice. Your manager approves or declines requests within three business days. Add approved PTO to your Google Calendar as an out-of-office event.

## Carryover

Up to 5 unused PTO days carry over into the next calendar year. Any balance above 5 days is forfeited on January 1. Carried-over days must be used by March 31.

## Holidays and Sick Leave

Corvane Labs observes 11 paid company holidays each year, plus a company-wide winter break between December 24 and January 1. Holidays do not count against your PTO balance.

Sick leave is separate from PTO. Employees receive 10 sick days per year, which do not carry over.
