# Parental Leave

Owner: People Team · Applies to: all full-time employees

## Eligibility

All full-time employees are eligible for parental leave from their first day of employment. The benefit applies to birth, adoption, and foster placement, and is the same for every parent regardless of gender.

## Leave Amount

Primary caregivers receive 16 weeks of fully paid leave. Secondary caregivers receive 8 weeks of fully paid leave. Leave must be taken within 12 months of the birth or placement and can be split into up to two blocks.

## How to Apply

Notify your manager and submit a parental leave request in Workday at least 30 days before your planned start date when possible. The People Team will confirm your dates and explain how leave interacts with any state disability or family leave programs within five business days.

## Returning to Work

Employees returning from parental leave may work a reduced schedule of 60 percent for the first four weeks back at full pay. Lactation rooms are available in every office and can be booked through the room booking system.
