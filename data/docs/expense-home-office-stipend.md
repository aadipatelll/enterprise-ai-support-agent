# Home Office Stipend

Owner: Finance Team · Applies to: hybrid and fully remote employees

## Stipend Amount

Fully remote employees receive a one-time home office stipend of $750 during their first 90 days. Hybrid employees receive a one-time stipend of $400. Office-based employees are not eligible. The stipend renews every three years.

## Eligible Purchases

Eligible items include a desk, an ergonomic chair, a monitor arm, lighting, and noise-reducing equipment. Monitors, keyboards, mice, and headsets are not covered by the stipend because IT provides them through the IT portal.

## Internet Reimbursement

Remote and hybrid employees can claim up to $50 per month toward home internet service. Submit the claim in Expensify once per quarter with your internet bill attached.

## Claims and Coworking

Submit home office purchases in Expensify using the Home Office category within 30 days of purchase. Items purchased with the stipend belong to the employee and do not need to be returned when leaving the company.

Fully remote employees who live more than 50 miles from a Corvane Labs office may expense a coworking membership of up to $300 per month with manager approval.
