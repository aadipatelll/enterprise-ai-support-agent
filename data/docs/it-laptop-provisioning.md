# Laptop Provisioning

Owner: IT Department · Applies to: new hires and employees due for a refresh

## Standard Hardware

Engineers receive a 16-inch MacBook Pro with 36 GB of memory. All other employees receive a 14-inch MacBook Air or, on request, a Lenovo ThinkPad T14 running Windows 11. Every laptop ships with full-disk encryption, the endpoint protection agent, and the device management profile already installed.

## New Hire Timeline

Managers must submit the new hire hardware request in the IT portal at least 10 business days before the start date. Laptops are shipped to remote employees to arrive two business days before their first day. Office-based employees pick up their laptop from the IT desk on the morning of their first day.

## Refresh Cycle

Laptops are replaced every three years. IT emails you 30 days before your refresh date with ordering instructions. Early replacement is approved only for hardware failure or a documented change in job requirements.

## Accessories

Each employee may order one external monitor, a keyboard, a mouse, and a headset through the IT portal at no cost. Docking stations are available for employees who work in an office at least three days per week.
