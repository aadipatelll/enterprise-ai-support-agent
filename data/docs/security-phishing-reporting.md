# Phishing Reporting

Owner: Security Team · Applies to: all employees

## Recognizing Phishing

Phishing messages often create urgency, ask you to sign in through a link, request gift cards or wire transfers, or come from addresses that look similar to real ones, such as corvane-labs-support.com. Corvane Labs IT will never ask for your password or MFA code by email, chat, or phone.

## How to Report

Use the Report Phish button in Gmail to report any suspicious email. The button sends the message to the Security team and removes it from your inbox. For suspicious texts, calls, or Slack messages, post a screenshot in the #security-help channel. Do not forward phishing emails to colleagues.

## If You Clicked

If you clicked a link, opened an attachment, or entered credentials, report it immediately in #security-help and call the IT service desk at extension 4357. Change your password right away. You will not be disciplined for reporting a mistake quickly; late reporting is the bigger risk.

## Training

Security runs simulated phishing campaigns every month. Employees who click a simulated phishing link are assigned a 10-minute refresher training module.
