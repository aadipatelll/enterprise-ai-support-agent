# Corporate Card Policy

Owner: Finance Team · Applies to: employees issued a Corvane Labs corporate card

## Eligibility

Corporate cards are issued to employees who travel at least four times per year or who manage team budgets. Request a card through the Finance section of the IT portal with your manager's approval. Cards arrive within 10 business days.

## Limits

The standard monthly limit is $5,000. Limits up to $15,000 are available with department head approval. Single purchases over $3,000 require a purchase order instead of the card.

## Allowed Use

Use the corporate card only for business expenses that follow the travel, meals, and expense policies. Personal purchases are never allowed, even if you plan to repay them. Cash withdrawals are blocked.

## Reconciling Charges and Lost Cards

Each card transaction appears in Expensify automatically. Add a receipt and business purpose to every charge within 15 days. Cards with transactions unreconciled for more than 30 days are frozen until they are resolved.

Report a lost or stolen card immediately in the card provider's mobile app and notify Finance in the #ask-finance Slack channel. A replacement card arrives within 5 business days.
