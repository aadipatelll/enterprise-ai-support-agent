# Expense Reporting

Owner: Finance Team · Applies to: all employees who incur business expenses

## Submitting Expenses

Submit all expenses in Expensify within 30 days of the purchase date. Expenses submitted after 90 days are not reimbursed. Each expense needs a category, a business purpose, and a receipt for any amount of $25 or more. Itemized receipts are required for meals.

## Approval and Reimbursement

Expense reports are approved by your direct manager. Reports totaling more than $2,500 also require approval from the department head. Finance audits a random sample of reports every month.

Approved expenses are reimbursed by direct deposit within 7 business days of final approval. Check your Expensify account for the status of each report.

## Missing Receipts

If you lose a receipt, submit a missing receipt declaration in Expensify. You may use no more than three missing receipt declarations per calendar year.

## Common Rejection Reasons

The most common reasons for rejected expenses are missing receipts, missing attendee names for meals, personal expenses mixed into a report, and duplicate submissions of a corporate card charge. Fix the issue and resubmit within 10 days.
