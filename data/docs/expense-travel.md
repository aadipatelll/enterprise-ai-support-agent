# Business Travel Policy

Owner: Finance Team · Applies to: all employees traveling for work

## Booking Travel

Book all flights, hotels, and rental cars through Navan, the company travel platform. Bookings made outside Navan are not reimbursed unless they are at least 15 percent cheaper and approved by your manager in advance. Book flights at least 14 days before departure when possible.

## Flights

Economy class is required for flights under 6 hours. Premium economy is allowed for flights of 6 hours or more. Business class requires approval from a vice president. Checked bag fees for one bag are reimbursed.

## Hotels

Hotel rates are capped at $250 per night in most US cities and $350 per night in New York, San Francisco, Boston, and London. Stays above the cap require manager approval before booking.

## Ground Transportation and Approval

Use ride-share, taxis, or public transit for local travel. Rental cars are allowed when they are cheaper than ride-share for the trip. Mileage for a personal car is reimbursed at the current IRS rate.

All international travel requires approval from your manager and the Finance team at least 21 days before departure.
