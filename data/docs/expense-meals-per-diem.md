# Meals and Per Diem

Owner: Finance Team · Applies to: employees on business travel and hosting business meals

## Travel Per Diem

When traveling overnight for work, employees receive a meal per diem of $75 per day in the United States and $90 per day internationally. On the first and last day of travel, the per diem is 75 percent of the daily amount. Per diem is paid automatically through Navan based on your trip dates, so receipts are not required for your own meals.

## Client Meals

Meals with customers or partners are reimbursed at up to $100 per person, including tax and tip. List every attendee and their company in the expense report. Alcohol is reimbursable only at client meals and must not exceed 30 percent of the bill.

## Team Meals

Managers may host one team meal per quarter at up to $50 per person. Team meals must be approved in advance by the department head.

## Not Reimbursable

Meals during local, same-day work are not reimbursed unless they are client meals. Minibar charges, room service fees above the per diem, and meals for family members are not reimbursable.
