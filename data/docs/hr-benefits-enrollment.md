# Benefits Enrollment

Owner: People Team · Applies to: full-time employees in the United States

## When to Enroll

New employees must enroll in benefits within 30 days of their start date through the Benefits portal in Workday. Coverage starts on the first day of the month after your start date. If you miss the window, you can only enroll during open enrollment or after a qualifying life event.

## Open Enrollment

Open enrollment runs every year from November 1 to November 15. Changes take effect on January 1. During open enrollment you can change medical plans, add or remove dependents, and update flexible spending account elections.

## Plans Offered

Corvane Labs offers three medical plans: a PPO, an HMO, and a high-deductible plan with a health savings account. The company pays 100 percent of employee premiums and 80 percent of dependent premiums. Dental and vision coverage are included at no cost. The company contributes $1,000 per year to the HSA for employees on the high-deductible plan.

## Qualifying Life Events

Marriage, birth, adoption, or loss of other coverage allow you to change benefits within 30 days of the event. Upload documentation in Workday.
