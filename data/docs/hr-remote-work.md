# Remote and Hybrid Work Policy

Owner: People Team · Applies to: all employees

## Work Arrangements

Corvane Labs supports three arrangements: office-based (four or more office days per week), hybrid (two to three office days), and fully remote. Your arrangement is agreed with your manager and recorded in Workday. Hybrid employees should be in the office on Tuesdays and Wednesdays, which are team collaboration days.

## Working From Another Location

You may work from another location within your home country for up to 30 days per calendar year without approval. Working from a different country for any length of time requires approval from the People Team and Legal at least 21 days in advance because of tax and immigration rules.

## Core Hours

All employees should be available between 10:00 AM and 3:00 PM in their local time zone for meetings and collaboration. Outside core hours you may set your own schedule with your manager's agreement.

## Home Workspace

Remote and hybrid employees must work from a private space where confidential conversations cannot be overheard. See the Home Office Stipend policy for equipment reimbursement.
