# Email and Calendar Guidelines

Owner: IT Department · Applies to: all Google Workspace users

## Mailbox Limits

Each Corvane Labs mailbox has 50 GB of storage. Attachments larger than 25 MB are blocked; share large files as Google Drive links instead. Email older than seven years is deleted automatically under the data retention policy unless it is under legal hold.

## Shared Mailboxes

Teams can request a shared mailbox such as support@ or billing@ through an IT service desk ticket. Each shared mailbox needs two named owners who approve membership requests. Shared mailboxes are reviewed every quarter during access reviews.

## Calendar Etiquette

Keep your working hours and time zone up to date in Google Calendar so colleagues can schedule meetings correctly. Meetings longer than 30 minutes should include an agenda in the invite. Focus time blocks marked as "Do not disturb" automatically decline new meeting requests.

## External Forwarding

Automatic forwarding of company email to personal or external accounts is disabled and may not be configured by any employee. If you need to share a message with a vendor, forward it manually after checking that it contains no Confidential or Restricted data.
