# Runbook: Account Lockout

Owner: IT Department · Audience: employees and IT service desk

## Why Accounts Lock

Your Corvane account locks automatically after 10 failed sign-in attempts within 15 minutes. Common causes are an old password saved on a phone mail app, a mistyped password after a recent change, or repeated MFA denials.

## Self-Service Unlock

Accounts unlock automatically after 30 minutes. To unlock sooner, go to id.corvane.example, choose "Unlock account", and verify your identity with Okta Verify. Self-service unlock is available only if you have MFA enrolled.

## Service Desk Unlock

If self-service does not work, call the IT service desk at extension 4357. The agent will verify your identity with a video call and your employee ID before unlocking your account. Service desk agents will never ask for your password.

## Preventing Repeat Lockouts

After changing your password, update it on every device that uses it, including your phone's mail and calendar apps. If your account locks more than three times in one week without explanation, the Security team investigates it as a possible password-guessing attack and will contact you.
