# Multi-Factor Authentication Setup

Owner: IT Department · Applies to: all accounts with access to company systems

## Who Needs MFA

Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory for every Corvane Labs account, including email, the VPN, GitHub, and the HR portal. Accounts without MFA enrolled within 7 days of creation are suspended automatically.

## Approved Methods

The preferred method is the Okta Verify app with push notifications. Hardware security keys (YubiKey 5 series) are required for administrators and engineers with production access; request one through the IT service desk. SMS codes are allowed only as a temporary backup for 30 days while you wait for a hardware key or a new phone.

## Enrolling a Device

Open id.corvane.example, select Settings, then Security Methods, and choose Set up next to Okta Verify. Scan the QR code with the app on your phone and approve the test push. Enroll at least two methods so you are not locked out if you lose one device.

## Lost or New Phone

If you lose your phone or switch to a new one, contact the IT service desk by phone at extension 4357. After verifying your identity with your manager on a video call, IT will reset your MFA enrollment within one business day.
