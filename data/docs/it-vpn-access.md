# VPN Access

Owner: IT Department · Applies to: all employees working outside a Corvane Labs office

## When to Use the VPN

The GlobalProtect VPN is required to reach internal systems such as the self-service password portal, the finance system, the internal wiki, and staging environments. Email, Slack, and Google Drive work without the VPN. Connect to the VPN whenever you use public Wi-Fi, even for public websites.

## Installing the Client

GlobalProtect is installed automatically on company laptops. If it is missing, install it from the Self Service app on macOS or the Company Portal app on Windows. Use the portal address vpn.corvane.example and sign in with your Corvane credentials and MFA.

## Gateways

Choose the gateway closest to you: US-East (Boston), US-West (Seattle), or EU (Dublin). The client selects the best gateway automatically, but you can change it from the gateway menu if a connection feels slow.

## Limits

VPN sessions disconnect after 12 hours and must be re-authenticated. Split tunneling is enabled, so video calls do not go through the VPN. Personal devices are not allowed to connect to the VPN under any circumstances. For connection problems, follow the VPN troubleshooting runbook before contacting the service desk.
