# Code of Conduct

Owner: People Team and Legal · Applies to: all employees, contractors, and interns

## Expected Behavior

Corvane Labs expects everyone to treat colleagues, customers, and partners with respect. Harassment, discrimination, and retaliation are not tolerated in any form, whether in the office, at company events, or in online channels such as Slack.

## Conflicts of Interest

Disclose any outside job, board seat, or investment in a competitor, customer, or vendor through the conflict of interest form in Workday. Employees may not accept gifts from vendors worth more than $100 in total per year. Gifts above that value must be declined or reported to Legal.

## Reporting Concerns

Report concerns to your manager, your HR business partner, or the anonymous Ethics Hotline at ethics.corvane.example. The hotline is operated by an independent third party and is available 24 hours a day. Corvane Labs prohibits retaliation against anyone who reports a concern in good faith.

## Acknowledgement

All employees must read and acknowledge the Code of Conduct in Workday during onboarding and again every year in January. Violations may lead to disciplinary action up to and including termination.
