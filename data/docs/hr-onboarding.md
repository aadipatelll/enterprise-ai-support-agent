# New Employee Onboarding

Owner: People Team · Applies to: new hires and their managers

## Before Day One

New hires receive a welcome email from the People Team one week before their start date with login instructions and the first-week schedule. Managers must assign an onboarding buddy from the same team before the start date.

## First Week

Day one starts at 9:30 AM local time with a welcome session, followed by IT setup, MFA enrollment, and benefits overview. During the first week, every new hire must complete security awareness training and sign the code of conduct acknowledgement in Workday. Payroll and tax forms must be submitted by the end of day three.

## First 90 Days

Managers and new hires agree on a 30-60-90 day plan during the first week. Check-ins happen at 30, 60, and 90 days, and the 90-day check-in is recorded in Workday. Engineers ship a small production change in their first two weeks with support from their buddy.

## Getting Help

Questions about onboarding go to the #ask-people Slack channel. Questions about laptops or accounts go to the IT service desk.
