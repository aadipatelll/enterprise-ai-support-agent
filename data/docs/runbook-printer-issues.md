# Runbook: Office Printer Issues

Owner: IT Department · Audience: office-based employees

## Adding a Printer

Office printers are added automatically through the device management profile and named by office and floor, for example BOS-3-Color. If a printer is missing, open the Self Service app on macOS or Company Portal on Windows and install the Printers package for your office.

## Print Jobs Not Releasing

All printers use secure release. Send your job, then tap your badge on the printer's card reader to release it. Jobs are held for 24 hours before they are deleted. If your badge is not recognized, register it once by entering your Corvane username at the printer and tapping the badge again.

## Paper Jams and Toner

Clear paper jams by following the animation on the printer screen. Spare paper and toner are stored in the supply cabinet next to each printer. Do not open the printer's rear panel or fuser unit.

## Escalation

If a printer shows an error code or prints blank pages after a toner change, open an IT service desk ticket with the printer name and error code. Printer vendor repairs are usually completed within two business days.
