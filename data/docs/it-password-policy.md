# Password Policy

Owner: IT Department · Applies to: all Corvane Labs employees and contractors

## Password Requirements

Every Corvane Labs account password must be at least 14 characters long. Use a passphrase of four or more unrelated words rather than a short complex password. Passwords may not contain your name, username, or the word "Corvane". Reusing any of your last 10 passwords is blocked automatically by the identity system.

## Rotation and Expiry

Passwords for standard accounts expire every 180 days. Privileged administrator accounts expire every 90 days. You will receive email reminders 14 days and 3 days before expiry. If a password is suspected to be compromised, change it immediately and report it to the Security team through the #security-help Slack channel.

## Password Manager

All employees must store work credentials in 1Password, which is provisioned automatically on your first day. Never store passwords in browser autofill, spreadsheets, or sticky notes. Shared team credentials belong in a shared 1Password vault owned by a team lead, not in chat messages or email.

## Changing Your Password

Change your password at the self-service portal at id.corvane.example. You must be on the corporate network or connected to the VPN to use the portal.
